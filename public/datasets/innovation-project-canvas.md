## Hour 2 · Sprint 1 · Understand the user

- The user: who they are, in a sentence, with no name or identifying detail:
- How they do it now, step by step:
- The pain points, and which one hurts most:
- My evidence: what I asked, who I asked as Person A, Person B, and what they said:
- The constraints: time, money, permission, skills, what the training centre allows:

- Think about it: What did the person actually need, as opposed to what they first asked for?

## Hour 3 · Sprint 2 · Design options

- Solution option:
- Uses AI / no AI:
- Why it fits the problem, and what it costs in complexity:
- Chosen, or rejected because…:

(3 rows minimum.)

- Think about it: Would this problem still be worth solving without AI?

## Hour 4 · Sprint 2 · Responsible design

- The data flow: what goes in, what the solution does with it, what comes out, and what is stored:
- The human decision points: what a person must decide, check or approve:
- The failure modes: what could go wrong, for whom, and how badly:
- The safeguards: what stops each failure mode, or limits it:
- My success criteria, written before I build: how I will know it worked, and what would count as it not working:

- Think about it: What happens when the AI is wrong?

## Hour 5 · Sprint 3 · Build the prototype

- My biggest assumption, and why the prototype tests it:
- What I built, in a sentence, and which form I chose:
- What already breaks when I try it myself:
- What it cannot do yet, and what I left out on purpose:

- Think about it: What's the smallest prototype that can test your biggest assumption?

## Hour 6 · Sprint 3 · Test

- Tester (Person A, Person B…):
- What they were asked to do:
- What worked:
- Where they got confused:
- What failed:
- The one thing I did not expect:

(3 rows minimum.)

- Think about it: What evidence shows your change actually improved things?

## Hour 7 · Sprint 4 · Improve and red-team

- The change I made, or the risk I found:
- The test evidence or the attack that found it:
- What I changed, or the safeguard I added:
- What is still wrong, or still possible, after that:

(5 rows minimum.)

- Think about it: Which decision must stay with a human?
