Red-team prompt cards (Chapter 4, Generative AI & Prompting, session "Red-team a prompt")

Use one of these if you have no partner's prompt to red-team. For the prompt you pick, find one ambiguity, one
missing constraint and one claim in its likely answer that would need checking. Run it in the tool if you can;
you can also red-team it on paper by asking what the most likely continuation would be.

=== Prompt 1 ===
Write a short piece for the school newsletter about the history of our town, and include the year it was founded and its population.

=== Prompt 2 ===
Explain why electric cars are better for the environment, with some statistics to back it up.

=== Prompt 3 ===
Make me a two-week revision plan for my exams with the best memory techniques, backed by research.

Then apply the same three checks to your own reusable prompt template.
