How your project will be judged (Chapter 8, AI Innovation Project)

Read them before you start, not after. These are the same six criteria that appear at the top of the chapter page.

=== Problem ===
Getting started: Problem is identifiable.
Getting there: Problem and user are clearly defined with evidence.
Going further: Problem is well-bounded, evidence-based and compared with other ways of framing it.

=== Solution choice ===
Getting started: You have a solution idea.
Getting there: You explain why your approach fits the problem.
Going further: You compare AI and non-AI options and justify the simplest approach that works.

=== Prototype ===
Getting started: A basic demonstration.
Getting there: The prototype tests a core assumption.
Going further: The prototype is designed around measurable success and failure criteria.

=== Testing ===
Getting started: You got feedback.
Getting there: You recorded test evidence and made a change.
Going further: You ran structured tests, red-teamed failure modes and compared versions.

=== Responsible AI ===
Getting started: You named a risk.
Getting there: You identified privacy, bias, hallucination or oversight issues and a safeguard.
Going further: You analysed trade-offs, remaining risk, who's affected and who's accountable.

=== Presentation ===
Getting started: You described the idea.
Getting there: You communicated problem, prototype, test and learning clearly.
Going further: You used evidence, acknowledged uncertainty and handled challenge questions thoughtfully.
